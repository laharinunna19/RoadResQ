# 🚗 ROADRESQ

### 🆘 Help When Your Vehicle Leaves You Stranded

ROADRESQ is a smart roadside assistance platform designed to connect stranded vehicle owners with nearby roadside service providers.

## 🚨 The Problem

Vehicle breakdowns can happen anywhere and anytime. Finding a reliable mechanic or roadside service during an emergency can be difficult, especially at night, on highways, or in unfamiliar locations.

## 💡 Our Solution

ROADRESQ provides a simple platform where users can:

* Select their vehicle problem
* Share their current location
* Request roadside assistance
* Connect with nearby service providers
* Track the assistance request

## 🛠️ Services

⛽ Fuel Delivery
🛞 Flat Tyre Assistance
🔋 Battery Assistance
🔧 Vehicle Breakdown Support

## ✨ Key Features
